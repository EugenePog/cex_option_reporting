① Price with strategy boxes

Right of the chart: PnL at expiry if the price ends at that level — the hedged amount Q (Position hedged of each box, Box builder) with the open options of the boxes in the legend (switch a box off there and its Q and options drop out), from the current price. Premiums & fees (open): open options counted from their entry, with the premium received / paid and fees; Realized (closed): + realized PnL of options closed in the period — both in USD at the index price of each trade.
